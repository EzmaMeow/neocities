Note: this is more of a character theme than a character.

Mew the Stray is a title or name for some of my feline characters who do not act like their typical race. The act like a mix of a cat and what ever culture they were rasied in. They usally do not speak much except for meows and cat noises. They also tend to rather befriend the local stray cats than other humanoids and usally have a history of growing up in the streets ~~and raised by stray cats~~.

They then to play the role of a rogue or stealth based character ~~though they may play druid nature, or hunter like classes~~, thoug they are not too serious about the role except to surive or help her friends. Their ideal weapops and tools are bows, fishing and trapping gear, and unarmed. They are not afraid of using magic, but might not have acess to easly case magic.

They enjoy being lazy with the cats, following their curiosity, exploring ~~usally high places~~, fishing and trapping, and eating.
